Process

How AgroShield Works

From photo to full diagnosis in under 10 seconds.

1
📷 Take or Upload a Photo
Use your phone or camera to photograph your crop or plant. A clear, well-lit photo of the leaves, stem, or affected area works best. No special equipment needed.
2
🧠 AI Analyzes the Image
Our AI model, built on Claude (Anthropic's advanced AI), processes the image. It's trained on thousands of agricultural datasets covering diseases, pests, and plant conditions across all major crops.
3
📊 Get Your Full Report
In seconds, receive a detailed health score, pest identification, disease name, severity level, and exactly which insects or pathogens are present — with visual confidence percentages.
4
💡 Follow the Solution Plan
Receive a step-by-step treatment plan with organic and chemical options, application timing, dosage, and precautions. Seasonal care tips are included automatically based on your crop type.
5
🛒 Shop Directly for Products
Click the product links we generate — they take you directly to Amazon, Flipkart, or local agri-stores with the exact fertilizer, pesticide, or tool your plant needs. Budget-friendly options always included.

Year-Round Care

Seasonal Crop Guide

Know the critical windows for every season — what to do and what to watch for.

🌱
Spring (Feb–May)
Ideal for planting. Begin fertilizing, watch for early aphid and whitefly invasions. Soil preparation is key.
⚠️ Risk: Powdery Mildew, Aphids
☀️
Summer (May–Aug)
High water demand. Watch for heat stress, spider mites, and caterpillar outbreaks. Mulch to retain moisture.
⚠️ Risk: Spider Mites, Heat Stress
🍂
Monsoon / Kharif (Jun–Oct)
Peak risk for fungal disease and root rot. Ensure drainage, reduce watering, spray preventive fungicide.
⚠️ Risk: Blight, Root Rot, Fungus
❄️
Winter (Nov–Jan)
Protect from frost. Slow watering cycle, prune dead growth, apply potassium for cold resistance.
⚠️ Risk: Frost Damage, Slow Growth
